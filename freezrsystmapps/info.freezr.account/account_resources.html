Connect a Gmail or Microsoft Account

Authorize freezr to access external services on your behalf. OAuth accounts (Gmail) use "Connect Account"; IMAP mailboxes (Yahoo, iCloud, Fastmail, self-hosted) use "Add IMAP Mailbox" with an app password. The mail app works the same way for both.

Add IMAP Mailbox
